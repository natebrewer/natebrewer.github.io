Naloxone Carrying Tube

Project Overview - Protocol Test

  • Main Tube Material: 410 Stainless
  • Cap Material: 5051 Aluminum
  • Sealing: O-Rings
  • Shock Resistance: Closed Cell Foam Inserts

I built this as a side project to store the two vials of IM Narcan that I typically carry. I added the knurling to each end cap to help add some texture for removal with gloves, and then knurled the center of the tube for the aesthetic. Each vial has a closed cell foam insert in its respective slot, helping insulate from mechanical shock. There is no direct contact between the glass vial and metal walls to (hopefully) prevent breaking. I built this over the course of a few days at work from scrap metal.
